# Code of Conduct

Dev Context should be a practical, respectful project for everyone who contributes.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep technical criticism focused on the work.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not share secrets, tokens, or private project details.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate these expectations.
